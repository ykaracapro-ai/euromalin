About EuroMalin

A site that compares, tests and recommends the best ways to save money online. Neither bank nor influence. Just real, verified good deals.

Why this site exists

Cashback, promo codes and shared subscriptions have existed for years. Yet most people pay a lot of money on everything, just because nobody showed them the right reflexes in one clear place.

EuroMalin was born from a simple observation: affiliate sites are either too commercial (push any offer) or too technical (well done but inaccessible). On voulait un entre-deux : un site utile, transparent et qui respecte ses visiteurs.

How we select offers

Each offer published on EuroMalin is subject to 5 criteria:

  1. Real gain — How much does the user actually save or recover?
  2. Ease — How many clicks/minutes to activate the offer?
  3. Reliability — Has the platform existed for at least 1 year? Does it have verifiable customer reviews?
  4. Risk of cancellation — Is cashback often refused? Does the offer have any pitfalls?
  5. Actual usefulness — Is the offer useful to a normal user, not just an early adopter?

We never recommend an offer that we wouldn't use ourselves.

How we make money

EuroMalin is an affiliate site. This means that:

  • We receive a commission if you click on an affiliate link and take an action (registration, purchase, activation).
  • You don't pay anything extra — the price is the same for you, whether you go through our link or directly.
  • Our recommendations are independent — a commission does not make us recommend a mediocre offer.
  • We clearly indicate when a link is affiliated (badge 🔗 or “affiliate link” mention).
