Premium cosmetics are expensive, but at 50% it changes everything. Problem: How can you be sure you're not wrong about the product or the colour? Here's how to choose foundation and serum without regret.

1. Choose your foundation

The 3 types of finish

  • Matte → Oily or combination skin. Lasts all day without shine. Be careful of dry areas that mark.
  • Radiant / Light → Normal to dry skin. Gives shine. Avoid if you have oily skin (“oil” effect at the end of the day).
  • Natural / Satin → The right environment. All skin, every day.

Pitfalls to avoid

  • ❌ Buy a serum without knowing your skin type — too rich a serum on oily skin = guaranteed breakouts.
  • ❌ Choose the shade at random — a foundation that is too dark is unforgiving. Test or order 2 shades.
  • ❌ Pay full price for premium care — Amazon promotions are 50% off regularly. Be patient.
  • ❌ Mixing too many active ingredients — retinol + vitamin C + peptides together = irritation. Alternate care.
