GTA 6: what is officially confirmed

The real timeline

Grand Theft Auto VI launches November 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar has also scheduled an extended look for August 27 at 3 PM ET. No PC date is official as of this update.

The story follows Jason and Lucia across Vice City and the fictional state of Leonida. PlayStation currently describes GTA VI as a single-player experience, playable offline and exclusive to PS5 within Sony's console family. The base game therefore does not require PlayStation Plus.

GTA 6 leaks: confirmed, plausible or pure rumor?

The 2022 breach: authenticated by Rockstar

In September 2022, a network intrusion led to stolen development footage being shared online. Rockstar confirmed the incident. The footage came from an early build, so it proved the project existed but not that every mechanic would survive to launch.

The August 2026 clips: still unconfirmed

Since August 18, alleged clips and map images have circulated again. Reports describe basketball, a stamina meter, police identification mechanics and a six-star wanted level. Rockstar has not authenticated the material. Even if genuine, its capture date and relevance to the final build are unknown.

Our anti-rumor rule: we do not publish stolen files or link to the footage. Mechanics inferred from the new leaks are hypotheses, not promises. The official August 27 presentation will be the better benchmark.

Why mention GamsGo if GTA 6 is single-player?

Because GTA VI is only one part of owning a PS5. PlayStation Plus remains useful for multiplayer in other games, cloud saves, monthly games and, depending on the tier, the Extra or Premium catalogs. Do not buy PS Plus believing it is required for GTA VI's story: it is not.
