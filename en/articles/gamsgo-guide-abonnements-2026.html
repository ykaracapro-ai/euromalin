Shared subscriptions: limitations to know

A cheaper offer may be linked to family sharing, a managed profile or third party access. This can reduce control over the account, prevent certain changes or cause interruption if the group changes. Avoid saving sensitive data and use a unique password when creating access for yourself.

Marketplace and sellers

Certain categories are proposed as a marketplace. Compare the description, evaluations, method of delivery, and announced coverage. Do not continue a transaction outside the expected payment and courier: proof of order is useful if a support intervention becomes necessary.

Our method of choosing

  1. Define the service and duration actually required.
  2. Check the type of access, country and compatible devices.
  3. Return the price to a comparable monthly cost.
  4. Read the restrictions before transmitting personal information.
  5. Test WPQTU and check the total before paying.
