Which coffee machine to choose in 2026? Comparative Nespresso, Tassimo, Dolce Gusto

Nespresso Original, Vertuo, Tassimo, Dolce Gusto… Are you lost in pods and systems? Me too until I dig. Here's everything you need to know so you don't make a mistake.

3 questions to ask yourself before buying

  1. What coffee do you drink? Expresso serré, lungo, ou grand mug américain ?
  2. What is your pod budget? The real cost is there, not in the machine.
  3. How many coffees per day? 1 or 5 changes everything.

Case #1: The espresso purist

You drink 1 to 3 espressos a day, you want smooth cream and consistent quality coffee.

→ Go to Nespresso Original. Compatible pods (L’Or, Starbucks, etc.) cost €0.35–0.55 and the choice of roasts is immense. No need for Vertuo if you only drink espresso.

Case no. 2: The large-format drinker

You like 300 ml mugs in the morning, espresso after lunch, and sometimes an iced coffee.

→ Take the Nespresso Vertuo POP. This is the only one that offers 5 sizes including the mug (230 ml), the carafe (535 ml) and the expresso. Centrifusion creates an incomparable thick foam. At €59 with Aeroccino included, it's unbeatable.

Case no. 3: The varied family

You have an espresso, your wife a cappuccino, the children a hot chocolate.

→ Direction Tassimo. The pods include a barcode that automatically adjusts the quantity of water. Cappuccino, latte macchiato, Milka hot chocolate… At €36.78, it is the most versatile entry-level.

🔥 See the Bosch Tassimo Happy deal →

And Dolce Gusto?

Niche: capsules are as expensive as Nespresso for lower quality (no cream, more diluted coffee). The advantage is the variety of drinks (chai, matcha, etc.). Take only if you drink something other than coffee.

Pitfalls to avoid

  • ❌ Buy without looking at the price of the pods. Over 2 years, the pods cost 3 to 5x the price of the machine.
  • ❌ Take a machine that is incompatible with your cups. Vertuo POP requires a minimum height of 12 cm.
  • ❌ Forget about descaling. An undescaled machine lasts 6 months instead of 5 years.
  • ❌ Buy the full pot. French Days promotions are the best time. Otherwise, look at the good deals.
