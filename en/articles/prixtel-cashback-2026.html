FAQ — Prixtel Cashback

Is the Pricetel cashback compatible with a promo code?

Yes, in most cases. First activate the cashback via your platform, then apply the promo code when subscribing. Both can be combined.

No, the cashback is linked to an subscription, not an existing offer change. If you change your plan at Prixtel, it doesn't count as a new activation.
