Cashback crypto 2026: recover up to 9% on each purchase without trader

You can receive 3% to 9% cashback in cryptos on your daily expenses — groceries, gas, subscriptions, restaurants. Without buying or selling a single token. Find out how with Plutus, Binance and Curve.

Do you think crypto is too complicated, too risky, not for you? I agree — if we're talking about trading. But the cashback in crypto, it's another world. You spend normally, you receive a refund in tokens. Point. No purchases, no portfolio to manage, no risk of losing.

Some platforms do this. Some are excellent. Here are the best in 2026 for get money with cryptos.

Plutus — the king of crypto cashback (3% to 9%)

Plutus is a debit card that gives you 3% cashback in PLUS tokens on all your daily purchases. And you can go up to 9% depending on your subscription. This is the simplest option for get money with cryptos without trading.

How it works

  • You load your Plutus card (classic bank transfer, no need for crypto)
  • You spend normally: groceries, restaurant, gas, subscriptions
  • You receive 3% cashback in PLUS tokens on each transaction
  • You can use your PLUS to pay off Netflix, Spotify, Uber, and 50+ other services

“Perks”: the real bonus

The best of Plutos is the Perks. Every month you can choose a service (Netflix, Spotify, Disney+, Amazon Prime...) and make yourself refund 100% up to €10. So your Netflix subscription at €9.99 = free via cashback.

💰 Concrete example : with the free plan (3% cashback + 1 Perk), a family that spends 1000€/month can recover the equivalent of 30€ to 50€/month in tokens. Add the Perk Netflix (10€/month offered), you are close to 60€/month. It's one of the best cashback programs of the market.

Binance Card — cashback in BNB

The Binance card also offers cashback, but with a different logic:

  • Up to 8% cashback depending on your BNB staking level
  • Limit : Cashback is capped at €25/month on a free basis
  • Advantage : utilisable partout, retraits gratuits au distributeur
  • Disadvantage : the cashback depends on how many BNBs you store, which implies a prior purchase

For ordinary people, Plutus is simpler: no need to buy crypto before starting.

Curve + cashback cards — the ultimate accumulation

Curve is not a cashback card in itself, but a card aggregator. You plug your Plutus card into it and you accumulate the advantages:

  • Plutus cashback on your purchase (3-9%)
  • Cashback Curve on the same transaction (1%)
  • And even free exchange fees abroad

Result: up to 10% reimbursement on the same purchase by stacking services. A simple tip for get money with cryptos effortlessly.

Coinbase Card — simple but limited

Coinbase also offers a map with crypto cashback:

  • 1% to 4% depending on the crypto chosen
  • Choice of refund crypto for each purchase
  • No withdrawal fees
  • But the cashback is lower and the limits are low

Crypto cashback is not without pitfalls:

Where to start?

My advice: Pluto. It is the simplest, most accessible, without prior purchase of crypto. You download the app, you make a transfer, you spend. The tokens arrive automatically. For get money with cryptos, this is the ideal solution.

If you want to go crazy, add Curve on top to accumulate cashbacks. It's like stacking two promotions on the same purchase.

💡 The winning strategy : Plutos for the daily (courses, petrol) + Curve to accumulate refunds + 1 Perk Netflix. Without buying a single euro of crypto from your pocket.

Verdict

The crypto cashback is the classic cashback better: higher rates (3% instead of 1%), perks that reimburse your subscriptions, and an almost zero learning curve. If you spend more than 500€/month, it's definitely worth it.

Our advice : start with Plutos (3% everywhere, without purchasing crypto). Add Curve when you master. Drop the cards with staking if you don't want to buy crypto.
