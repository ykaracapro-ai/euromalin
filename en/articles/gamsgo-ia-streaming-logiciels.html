IA tools: choose according to usage

A general assistant is useful for writing, summarizing and searching for ideas. A specialized tool better responds to programming, music, image or video. Before paying, check the included version, generation limits, number of users, and degree of control over the account.

Streaming: quality, profiles and region

For video or music, look at image or sound quality, number of screens, presence of advertisements, profiles and regional catalogue. An offer accessible via a managed profile does not give the same control as a personal subscription. Do not use a shared profile to store private information.

Software: activation or access managed?

Office automation, creative or assembly tools can be delivered by activation, key, account or other mechanism. Check the version, compatible system, duration and ability to link your own address. For professional use, also make sure the conditions of licence match your activity.
