U7BUY, how does this work?

U7BUY is above all a marketplace. This means that price, time and quality of delivery often depend on an independent seller. The platform centralizes advertisements, payment, messaging and litigation, but two offers for the same product can present very different conditions.

The public catalogue includes reloads, objects, accounts, currencies, booster services, subscriptions and content related to Roblox. This width is convenient to compare, provided you never confuse abundance of choice with uniform guarantee.

The 7 controls to do before paying

  1. Read the title and all the description. Check the platform, region, duration, delivery mode and what is actually included.
  2. Compare multiple sellers. A small price difference can justify a better rated or more experienced seller.
  3. Watch history. Recent opinions, number of orders and detailed comments are more than an isolated note.
  4. Check the announced deadline. Fast delivery is not always instant.
  5. Read the rules of the publisher. A game or service may prohibit resale, account sharing, external currency or boosting.
  6. Stay on the platform. Refuse any payment or exchange moved to an external courier.
  7. Keep the evidence. Capture the ad, order and messages until the end of the warranty.
