Mistakes to avoid

Some common pitfalls when combining cashback and purchases on these platforms:

  • Use external promo code — certain codes cancel the cashback. First test without code, then compare.
  • Open multiple tabs — tracking may not work if you browse elsewhere before finalizing.
  • Wait too long — rates change often. If you see a good rate, validate your purchase immediately.
  • Do not check the T&Cs — certain categories (electronics, large household appliances) are excluded from cashback.
  • Forget to log in — make sure you are connected to your cashback account before each purchase.
