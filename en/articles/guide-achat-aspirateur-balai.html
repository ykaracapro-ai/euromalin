Broom vacuum cleaner: which one to choose without being trapped by marketing?

Battery life, power, weight, brush, animal hair, apartment or house: the simple buying guide for choosing a useful stick vacuum cleaner.

Photo: Unsplash

The broom vacuum cleaner has become an ultra-current purchase, but also a perfect ground for fuzzy marketing. Between the highlighted watts, the "offered" accessories, the huge autonomy announcements and the promises of absolute power, many people pay too much for a device not so convenient on a daily basis.

The best broom vacuum cleaner is not necessarily the most powerful on the product sheet. That's the one you want to go out without thinking. If it is too heavy, too noisy, discharges too quickly or is difficult to empty, you will take it less often. And a device that you don't use has no value, even if purchased on sale.

What to really compare

The first criterion, it's the weight. A stick vacuum cleaner must be practical. If you feel it heavy after two minutes, it will quickly annoy you. The second criterion, this is the useful autonomy, not the theoretical autonomy in eco mode. What matters is whether it can clean your real space without stress.

The third point, this is the main brush. If you have carpets, pets, long hair or a lot of fine dust, the vacuum head and brush maintenance matter a lot. A bad system gets clogged quickly and wastes your time.

You must also look at the container, the ease of emptying it, the possible wall mounting, and the ease of charging. These are details that really change the experience.

Apartment, house, animals: not all needs are equal

For a small apartment, a light and simple model is often more than enough. For a large surface area or a two-story house, autonomy and comfort of use become priorities. If you have animals, you should especially look at the effectiveness on the hair and the maintenance of the brush, not just the promise of power.

The right purchase depends more on your soil, your surface and your cleaning pace than on the brand's prestige.

The right budget

  • Entry level (~80-130€) : correct for light use and a small space. Example: Dream T20 or Philips Performer Max.
  • Mid-range (~150-300€) : the best balance between autonomy, comfort and efficiency. Example: Rowenta X-Force Flex 15.60 or Samsung Bespoke Jet.
  • High-end (350€+) : se justifie si tu nettoies souvent, grande surface, plusieurs types de sols ou contraintes spécifiques (poils d'animaux). Example: Dyson V15 Detect or Samsung Bespoke Jet AI.

* Prices are given for information only (April 2026). Always check the exact price of the desired model before purchasing.

Traps to avoid

Don't get hypnotized by numbers alone. Autonomy announced without context does not mean much. Same thing for the “ultra powerful” arguments if the comfort of use is not there.

Second mistake: buying an overly expensive model to replace a large sled when in reality you just want a quick backup device.
Third mistake: forgetting the cost or availability of filters, batteries or wearing parts.

My EuroMalin advice

Take a stick vacuum cleaner that you will use really often. Simplicity and comfort almost always win against the “impressive” technical sheet. And if you buy online, compare the final price, delivery and cashback before validating.

Who do I recommend it to?

  • Apartment or small house
  • Quick and frequent cleanings
  • People who want to avoid getting out a big vacuum cleaner
  • Homes with children or animals, if the model is suitable

When to buy?

Sales, household appliance sales, Black Friday, e-commerce flash offers, end of series when a new range arrives. And local fairs Some exhibitors offer discounts on exhibition models or lots with accessories.
