Faut-il passer par un MVNO (opérateur virtuel) ?

MVNOs like Prixtel, NRJ Mobile, YouPrice or Syma Mobile are often cheaper than large operators because they rent their network. The cashback is sometimes higher to compensate for their lower reputation.

Advantages : very low price, high cashback at activation, no commitment
Disadvantages : priorité réseau réduite, service client parfois limité

Mistakes to avoid

  • Paying too much for unused data : if you are still in Wi-Fi, a small 2€ package (Free) + a one-time data package may suffice
  • Forget cashback : tens of euros go in smoke at each subscription
  • Not checking coverage : a cheap package is useless if you don't have a network at home
  • Sign a commitment : packages without commitment allow to change as soon as a better promotion arrives

Verdict

The best mobile plan in 2026 depends on your consumption, but one rule applies to everyone: never subscribe without checking the cashback. Whether you choose Free for value for money, RED for data or Pricetel for the price, 10 to 20€ cashback for activation, it's always good to take.
