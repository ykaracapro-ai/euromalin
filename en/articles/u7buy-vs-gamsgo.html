Common risks

Both sites may offer shared accounts, accounts controlled by a third party, invitations, or products subject to a publisher's policies. This may result in a region check, loss of access, recovery by the original owner, or in-game punishment. For AI tools, emails, files, and histories may also be visible to other people.

Simple rule: no confidential document on a shared account, no off-platform payment, no confirmation of receipt before complete testing.
