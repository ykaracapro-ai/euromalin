Top up: the most direct format

A reload adds credit, currency or content to an account. Always check the country, server, platform and ID requested. Failure to enter can make correction difficult, as delivery is sometimes automatic.

Objects and currencies: attention to methodology

Delivery can be by exchange, hotel sales, internal mail or joint session. The announcement must explain the procedure. Before purchasing, check that your character meets the required requirements and that the method does not violate the rules of the game. An apparent economy does not compensate for the risk of suspension.

Accounts: the most sensitive product

An account may contain rare characters, rows or objects, but it presents a risk of recovery by the former owner. Analyze the advertised warranty period, editable information and seller history. U7BUY displays a 14 day account guarantee on its catalogue page; read the detailed conditions applicable to the chosen offer.

Boosting: security and confidentiality

Some services require access to the account, others a team game. Prefer the least intrusive method. If access is required, remove the registered means of payment, use a temporary password and change it after the service. Make sure the publisher allows the practice.
