1. Skyscanner: the master of cheap flights

Why Skyscanner is essential

Skyscanner is the most powerful flight comparator. It scrapes hundreds of online companies and agencies (Expedia, Opodo, eDreams, Kayak, etc.) and gives you the best price. Free, without intrusive ads, and with features that others don't have.

The 5 Skyscanner tips that make the difference

🔥 Tip #1: “Everywhere” search

You only know that you want to go in April, but not where? Instead of searching city by city, put “Everywhere” as a destination. Skyscanner brings you the cheapest flights around the world from your airport.

Example: Depuis Paris en mai, tu peux trouver Barcelone 39€, Lisbonne 49€, Marrakech 65€, Athènes 89€. At a glance.

🔥 Tip #2: the whole month

Skyscanner lets you search a whole month and shows you the cheapest days in a calendar. A Paris-Marrakech flight can go from €85 (departure Friday) to €49 (departure Tuesday). Either -42% just by changing the day.

🔥 Tip #3: Price Alerts

Do you have a destination in mind but no fixed date? Activate itSkyscanner alert. As soon as the price drops, you will receive a notification. The best prices are often 2 to 4 months before departure.

🔥 Tip #4: “Incognito” mode for low-cost airlines

Ryanair, EasyJet, Volotea: Skyscanner shows you the direct price but redirects you to their site. Caution: low-cost ones are not always in the direct comparator. Also check their site if Skyscanner doesn't show them.

🔥 Tip #5: Alternative airports

Skyscanner automatically includes secondary airports. Paris = CDG + ORY + BVA (Beauvais). London = LHR + LGW + STN (Stansted) + LTN (Luton). Sometimes an airport 1 hour away by train saves you €100.

Cabin baggage vs hold: the real calculation

  • Cabin baggage only (low-cost): free or 6-12€/trip if priority. For 2 round trips per year: €24-48.
  • Checked baggage: 25-45€/trip. For 2 AR: 100-180€.
  • Annual savings: 76 to 132€ just by taking a good cabin bag.

4. The 10 traveler tips that combine

  1. 🚆 Compare train vs plane — On domestic journeys (Paris-Lyon, Paris-Marseille), the train is sometimes cheaper + no airport transport. Check on Trainline or SNCF Connect.
  2. 📅 Avoid school holidays — Prices are soaring. If you can go in May, June or September, you save 30 to 50%.
  3. 💳 No-fee bank card — Some online banks (Revolut, Wise, N26) do not have exchange fees. Up to 3% savings per transaction abroad.
  4. 🏨 Booking Genius — The Booking loyalty program gives 10-20% discount after 5 reservations. Can be combined with iGraal cashback? No, the two are not compatible.
  5. 🌍 Rental between individuals — Airbnb, Booking.com and Vrbo. Sometimes cheaper than a hotel, especially in groups. Pay attention to cleaning costs.
  6. 🍽️ Avoid tourist restaurants — Eat where the locals eat. Street parallel to busy areas = -30% on the bill.
  7. 🎒 Cancellation insurance — Take out annual multi-trip insurance (20-40€/year) rather than for each reservation (often 5-10% of the amount).
  8. 🚕 On-site transportation — Uber/Bolt cheaper than a taxi at the airport. Also check transport passes (unlimited metro).
  9. 📱 eSIM travel — Airalo, Holafly: no roaming charges. Local Internet from €5 for a week.
  10. 💸 Combine the levers — Cheap flight (Skyscanner) + hotel with cashback (iGraal) + standard cabin baggage (Hayayu). Each lever is small, but the 3 together save €100-200 per trip.

Costly mistakes

  • ❌ Book the same day — Flights increase to D-7 and D-3. Buy between D-60 and D-30 for the best prices.
  • ❌ Take out travel insurance with each reservation — 15€ × 4 trips = 60€. Annual multi-trip insurance costs €25-40.
  • ❌ Forget cashback before booking — One more click on iGraal can bring in €20-50. Never book without it.
  • ❌ Change money at the airport — Catastrophic exchange rate. Uses a local ATM or Revolut/Wise card.
  • ❌ Take a taxi to the airport — Uber/Bolt is often 30 to 50% cheaper. Check both first.
