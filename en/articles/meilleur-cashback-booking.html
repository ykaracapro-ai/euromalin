Frequently asked questions about Booking cashback

Does the cashback Booking work for all reservations?

Yes, for most hotel and accommodation reservations on Booking. Check the offer conditions for exclusions (some non-refundable reservations may be excluded).

How long does it take to receive cashback?

Cashback generally appears within 24 to 72 hours of booking, but is often subject to a validation period (30 to 60 days) before being available for withdrawal.

Can I combine cashback with other offers?

Yes, you can cumulate cashback with Booking (Genius) loyalty offers or with promo codes, but not with other cashback platforms on the same purchase.
