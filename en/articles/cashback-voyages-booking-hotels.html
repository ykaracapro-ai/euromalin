Cashback hotels and travels : Booking, Hotels.com, Expedia, Airbnb (2026)

Travel is expensive, especially in high season. However, a simple reflex before each reservation can save you several tens of euros: activate cashback. Complete comparison of platforms and tips to maximize your earnings.

Booking, Hotels.com, Expedia, Airbnb: these platforms generate billions of euros of reservations each year. Yet, a large majority of travellers do not think of cashback before validating their stay. A costly mistake.

Here's everything you need to know to get money back on your travel bookings.

Hotels.com : loyalty program + cashback

Hotels.com offers its own loyalty program (one night offered every 10 nights booked). The trick is to cumulate this program with cashback.

  • iGrail : 3% to 6% on Hotels.com
  • eBuyClub : 2% to 5%
  • Widilo : 2% to 4%

Tip: Log in to your Hotels.com account first (to activate the loyalty program), then activate cashback from the platform, then book. You combine both advantages.

Expedia: interesting rates on flights + hotel

Expedia is very present on cashback platforms, especially on packaged offers (flight + hotel).

  • eBuyClub : 3% to 6% on full stays
  • iGrail : 2% to 5%
  • Widilo : 2% to 4%

Flight + hotel packages are often better reimbursed than separate bookings.

Airbnb: cashback possible but rarer

Airbnb is less present on cashback platforms. Some offers exist but rates are lower and conditions more restrictive.

  • iGrail : 1% to 3% depending on the period
  • eBuyClub : 1% to 2%

The Airbnb cashback is often limited to bookings over €100 and does not apply to service charges.

When to book to maximize cashback?

The timing of your reservation influences the cashback rate:

  • Low season (January-March, September-November): boosted rates, sometimes x2
  • Weekday vs. weekend : rates are often better on weekends on Booking
  • Flash sales : some platforms offer double cashbacks for 24 hours
  • Black Friday / Cyber Monday : the best rates of the year on all platforms

Mistakes to avoid when booking a trip

  • Do not compare platforms : rate can increase from 2% to 7% depending on where you activate the cashback
  • Book with free cancellation then change : if you modify or cancel, the cashback is lost
  • Use external promo codes : some codes cancel the cashback. Check the T&Cs before
  • Use the mobile app : tracking works better from a desktop browser

How to cumulate cashback + loyalty programs?

This is the winning strategy to maximize your earnings on travel:

  • Booking : Genius programme (reductions 10-20%) + cashback (2-6%)
  • Hotels.com : Welcom Rewards program (1 night offered/10) + cashback (3-6%)
  • Expedia : Expedia Rewards points + cashback (3-6%)
  • Airbnb : sponsorship credits + cashback (1-3%)

Always enable your loyalty account BEFORE you activate the cashback. Both work independently.

Verdict

Cashback travel is one of the most profitable, as the amounts involved are high. For a holiday budget of €1000 to €2000 per year, you can collect between €50 and €150 simply by activating the cashback before each reservation.

Our advice : for Booking and Hotels.com, use eBuyClub or iGraal. Check rates 24 hours before booking, and activate the offer just before finalizing your payment.
