Costly mistakes

  • Use a shared account for client files or emails.

Frequently asked questions

Which subscription to test first?

Choose a non-sensitive, short-term service with a clearly described method of access and protection.

Should you choose the lowest price?

No. Compare duration, plan, region, account control and guarantee before amount.

Can you use your own account?

Some offers work by reload or invitation, others deliver new identifiers. The form must state that.

How to limit the risks?

Do not use any reused passwords or confidential data and keep all evidence on the platform.
