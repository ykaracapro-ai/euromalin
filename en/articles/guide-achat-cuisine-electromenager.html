Small household appliances: rice cooker, Air Fryer, accessories

Two devices that really change your kitchen without blowing up your budget. Here's how to choose them and not get caught on accessories.

1. Choose the right rice cooker

Why buy one?

A rice cooker is the most underrated appliance in your kitchen. Perfect rice every time, without supervision, without sticking, without missing the cooking. And it is also used for steamed vegetables, quinoa, oats.

Important criteria

Capacity0.8L = 4 servings. 1.5L = 6-8 servings. 2L = 10+ servings.
CoatingNon-stick (required) preferably ceramic or PTFE without PFOA.
Keep warmAuto after cooking. Essential if you cook in advance.
Steam basketAllows you to cook vegetables and rice at the same time. Huge time saving.
Power300-400W is enough for 0.8L. 600W+ for large formats.

Case by case

  • Alone or as a couple → 0.8L cooker. €24.99 for the Russell Hobbs, it's perfect. See the good plan →
  • Family (4+) → Take a 1.5L with ceramic tank. Count 40-60€.
  • Sushi fanatic → Some cookers have a specific sushi mode. Otherwise, rinsing the rice before cooking is enough.

2. Air Fryer: do you need to buy parchment papers?

The Air Fryer Explained

An Air Fryer cooks your food with 80% less oil than a traditional fryer. Result: crispy fries, nuggets, roasted vegetables, and even cakes. The Cecotec is one of the best-selling models on Amazon (4.6/5).

Air Fryer Papers: useful or gadget?

Useful, clearly. Perforated parchment papers avoid:

  • That the food sticks to the basket
  • To have to rub the non-stick coating (which grazes)
  • Cleaning after each use

Figures: 150 papers at €4.90 = €0.03/piece. If you use the Air Fryer 3 times/week, that gives you 50 meals cleaned in 30 seconds for less than €2.

Pay attention to the diameter

Not all papers are compatible with all Air Fryer. Cecotec 150× work with 4-6L models (the most common). Check the diameter of your basket before purchasing.

3. The winning combo

Rice cooker + Air Fryer + papers = fast and healthy meal base. Perfect rice in the cooker, crispy vegetables/meat with Air Fryer. Total time: 20 minutes, cleaning: 1 minute. For less than 30€ of appliances.

Pitfalls to avoid

  • ❌ Buy a cooker without keeping warm — you will have to watch the cooking, which kills the interest.
  • ❌ Take too big — a 2L cooker for 1 person is bulky and you miss out on small quantities.
  • ❌ Use metal utensils in the cooker — guaranteed scratch on the coating.
  • ❌ Buy oversized Air Fryer papers — check the diameter of your basket before.
