Cashback food shopping : Leclerc, Carrefour, Auchan, Intermarket (Guide 2026)

There is little talk about cashback on food races — yet it is one of the most underestimated levers to reduce its monthly budget. Between drive, delivery and in-store discounts, here is what really exists to save up to €100 per year.

When we think of cashback, we often imagine clothing or high-tech purchases. However, food shopping represents the largest item of expenditure for French households — around €400 per month on average. Even a small percentage refund can make a real difference at the end of the year.

But be careful: the food cashback does not work everywhere and not in the same way. Here is the complete guide to maximize your refunds in 2026.

What rates for races?

Cashback on food shopping is generally lower than on clothing or high-tech. But it has an advantage: it applies to higher and more regular amounts.

  • Leclerc Drive : 1% to 3% depending on the platform and the period
  • Carrefour Drive : 1% to 2% on average
  • Auchan : 1% to 2.5%
  • Intermarché : 1% to 2%
  • Super U : 1% to 2%
💡 On a shopping budget of €400/month, 1% cashback = €48/year. Add the periods of boosted rates (back to school, holidays, food sales) and you can reach €80 to €100 in annual reimbursement.

In parallel with classic cashback platforms, racing apps also make it possible to recover money:

  • Too Good To Go — anti-waste baskets at reduced prices in supermarkets
  • Octopus — sometimes includes drive-thru food offers
  • Shopmium — refund on specific products in store
  • Koupon — discount vouchers + cashback on food

Limits to know

Food cashback has its particularities:

  • Drive is more reliable than delivery : tracking works better on orders placed online and removed in store
  • No cashback at checkout : refund applies only to orders prepared online (drive or delivery)
  • Frequent exclusions : fresh products, meat and fish are sometimes excluded from cashback
  • Minimum amount : some platforms impose a minimum order amount to activate cashback (often 20€)

How to optimize your shopping with cashback?

A few simple reflexes to ensure you don't miss anything:

  • Prepare your shopping list on the drive site, then activate cashback before paying
  • Compare rates between iGraal and eBuyClub: they can vary from one to two on the same brand
  • Take advantage of welcome offers : some platforms offer a bonus if you activate cashback on a drive as soon as you register
  • Combine with the brand's promotions : cashback is added to discounts and promo codes

Verdict

The cashback on food races will not make you a millionaire, but it is one of the most regular and simple levers to activate. If you already order in drive, just a few seconds to activate the offer and recover between 50 and 100€ per year.
