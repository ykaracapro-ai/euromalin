Risks to check before paying

Point specific to Claude: Do not handle any confidential information in a seller or shared account. Conversations and usage limits may be visible or consumed by others.
  • Private or shared: Who else can see history, files or profiles?
  • Control: can you access the email, change the password and recover the account?
  • Region: does access work in France or Belgium without bypass?
  • Duration: when does it start and what happens if access stops before then?
  • Warranty: How long does the seller replace or refund access?
  • Editor's Rules: Is resale, out-of-home sharing or account transfer permitted?
The cheapest is not always the most economical: an account that is not recoverable or replaced several times can cost more in terms of time, data and stress.
