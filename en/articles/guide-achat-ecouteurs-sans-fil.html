Which wireless headphones to choose? The useful guide to avoid overpaying for the brand

Autonomy, comfort, noise reduction, calls, sport, transport, budget: the simple guide to choosing good wireless headphones without overpaying the brand.

Photo: Unsplash

Wireless headphones are one of the purchases where many people pay mainly for the logo, the hype or the aesthetics. And yet, on this type of product, what really matters on a daily basis is much more concrete: comfort, stability, autonomy, call quality, noise reduction and ease of use.

The best model is not the most "premium" in absolute terms. This is the one that corresponds to your main use. Transport, work, sport, calls, music at home: it's not the same search.

The 5 criteria that really matter

The first, it’s comfort. Headphones that are excellent on paper but unpleasant after twenty minutes become a bad purchase. The second, it’s the outfit. If you move a lot, play sports or walk for a long time, stability is essential.

The third criterion, this is the call quality. Many users spend as much time talking as listening to music. If the microphones are mediocre, the experience drops very quickly.
The fourth, it's noise reduction, but only if you really need it. In transport or open spaces, that changes everything. At home, this is not always decisive.
The last, it's the real autonomy with the case and the ease of recharging.

Which type to choose according to your profile?

  • Simple daily use (music, podcasts, videos): you don't need the very high end.
  • Frequent calls : microphones and connection stability become priority.
  • Public transport - noise reduction is often worth the extra cost.
  • Sports : first seeks support, resistance and comfort.

The trap is buying an ultra expensive model "just in case", when your actual use often justifies a simpler segment.

The right budget

  • Entry level (~20-50€) : may be sufficient for occasional use, but the difference is often played on microphones, stability and comfort. Example: Soundcore Liberty 4 Pro (~50€).
  • Mid-range (~60-130€) : the smartest area. Good overall quality, pleasant use, without paying the maximum marketing tax. Example: Nothing Ear (~119€) or Samsung Galaxy Buds FE (~80€).
  • High-end (150€+) : relevant if you use your headphones every day, long, in the noise, or if you make a lot of calls. Example: Sony WF-1000XM6 (~280€) or Apple AirPods Pro 3 (~249€).

* Prices are given for information only (April 2026). Always check the exact price of the desired model before purchasing.

Mistakes to avoid

The first mistake is to buy only on design. The second is to ignore comfort. The third is to overpay a well-known brand while a better positioned model would do exactly the same job for your use.

Another trap: believe that great sound is enough. In real life, a good product also means a good case, simple connection, practical controls and a battery that doesn't bother you.

My EuroMalin advice

Choose according to your dominant use, not according to advertising. If you want a smart buy, target the segment where the experience is really good without paying only the name. And before ordering, always compare the final price with promo, cashback or good plan of the moment.

I recommend this type of product for:

  • Daily transportation
  • Hybrid work
  • Regular calls
  • Light sport
  • Simple and discreet mobile use

When to watch for a promotion?

Tech operations, Prime Day, French Days, sales, Black Friday, bundles with accessories. And high-tech salons : good deals on the models presented.
